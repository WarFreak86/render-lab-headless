**Owner decision recorded:** The owner approved using their own address for authorized returns. Support will provide the address privately, only in the individual authorization or prepaid return label. The address is never entered into storefront code, Shopify policies, deployment configuration, customer-facing templates, or a Shopify fulfillment location. Gelato remains the sole outbound fulfillment provider. Keep all fulfillment routing and Gelato shipping rates unchanged. Shopify Admin read-only Markets showed one active market, United States, with US as its sole region.

**Refund timing for final approval:** Issue approved refunds within **five business days** of receiving the return, or of approval if no return is needed, and sooner if required by law. Inspect within that same clock; do not restart it at inspection. Shopify documents that for [Shopify Payments refunds](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts/refunds) the customer may wait up to 10 business days to receive the funds, depending on the bank. These are separate clocks. Plan staffing for the five-day merchant commitment before approving it. Keep the proposed amount treatment: refund item and applicable tax; exclude original postage for standard returns where lawful, and include attributable original postage when refunding a verified fulfillment issue. Notify customers if an item fails inspection, document the reason, and honor mandatory remedies. The draft adds no extra mailing deadline after authorization.

**US legal checks:** [New York GBL §218-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/218-A) expressly covers online retailers and calls for conspicuous policy access near the product or before billing details, plus disclosure of conditions, proof of purchase, discount/as-is treatment, refund form, fees and written copies on request. The purchase panel and policy address those items. [Regulation Z §1026.12(e)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/12/#e) gives covered creditors other than the card issuer seven business days to transmit a credit after accepting a return; five business days from receipt is a more conservative store commitment. The [FTC's 16 CFR Part 435](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-435) separately governs delayed or unshipped online orders; where its prompt-refund requirement applies, use the legal trigger and deadline (often seven working days from when the refund right vests), not the voluntary returns clock. The 30-day defect claims process does not shorten implied warranty or other statutory rights; see the [Pennsylvania Attorney General consumer guide](https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/resources/brochures-publications/rights-and-resources-consumer-guide/). These checks are a US release review, not a determination for every state or product. Reassess before opening another market. The written authorization step must not delay a mandatory remedy.

**Private support workflow:** Verify order and delivery per item, date-stamp the request, assign a case ID, and decide whether the customer or Render-Lab covers postage. For approved standard returns, use the following reply in the verified customer's order thread:

> Your return for [item] on order [order number] is authorized. Please pack it securely in suitable original packaging and mail it with tracking to the address below. You purchase the return postage; there is no restocking fee. Reply with the tracking number and include case [case ID] in the package. We will inspect the item on arrival and issue an approved refund to your original payment method within five business days of receipt, or sooner if required by law.
>
> **Mail to:** [authorized support operator inserts the approved address here in this private reply only]

For a defect, wrong item, or damage, keep the 30-day email intake and open the Gelato claim promptly. If an item must be sent back, provide a prepaid label or other instructions that do not charge the customer. For no-return replacements, keep the existing Gelato fulfillment path. If the customer is eligible for a refund without return, log approval and issue it on the same five-day clock. Record inspection, refund amount and issue time. Gelato [does not accept ordinary returns or provide a returns address](https://support.gelato.com/en/articles/8996072-what-is-your-return-policy-and-quality-guarantee).

**Ethical influence design:** The decision is whether the artwork and return terms fit before purchase. Use verifiable policy as evidence, with a small link near Add to Cart and both the cost and the remedy written in plain language. Judge the change by return-related questions, approved returns and reasons, refund timing, complaints and net contribution after returns; track qualified conversion alongside these measures. No conversion claim or A/B test is justified without enough traffic.

## D. Wallets and delivery

- **Wallets:** Shopify Admin lists Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay in supported digital wallets. This is configuration evidence, not device-level checkout evidence. PayPal was not established by that query; check Settings → Payments and the provider status independently. Run US guest checkout checks before payment using Safari with an eligible Apple Pay wallet and a compatible browser/device with Google Pay; record visible options and check PayPal separately. Live storefront robots explicitly allows Storebot-Google on cart. Review checkout-domain access and whether a sign-in is required; do not infer a successful StoreBot checkout from storefront robots. Google [tracks PayPal, Google Wallet, Apple Pay and Amazon Pay](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14187023), and documents a validation period and support escalation for mismatches. Do not add wallet badges or enable providers on the strength of the score alone.
- **Delivery:** the live shipping policy quotes 2–4 business days of production for Gelato metal/canvas before transit. [Gelato's general guidance](https://support.gelato.com/en/articles/8996174-getting-started-with-shipping) explains that domestic Standard/Economy commonly takes 2–4 transit days and actual estimates depend on product, destination, capacity and method. That makes roughly 4–8 business days in a typical domestic scenario, before weekend/holiday and other exceptions; it does not disprove the reported ~10-day Merchant estimate. Obtain [Gelato's actual order estimates](https://support.gelato.com/en/articles/8996110-how-do-i-check-the-estimated-delivery-date-and-tracking-in-the-gelato-dashboard) and anonymized production/transit percentiles by size, material and US region. Compare Merchant handling, transit, cutoff, weekends and destinations to those observations. Keep current promises until the evidence supports a change.
- **Shipping cost:** Read-only Shopify Admin showed active US flat rates of $9.19 / $15.19 / $18.29 for small / medium / large canvas and $10.56 / $20.58 / $31.84 for small / medium / large metallic prints. These are profile rates, not complete checkout quotes for mixed carts or every destination. Even the lowest observed wall-art rate exceeds the reported ~$8 Good scorecard figure. Compare actual US ZIP and variant checkout quotes against Merchant service rates and item-level overrides. If Google advertises $8 for an affected offer, correct the Merchant shipping mapping to match checkout after separate approval. Keep checkout rates unchanged.
